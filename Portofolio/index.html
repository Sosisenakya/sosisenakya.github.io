About

I'm a Roblox developer focused on scripting and UI. Over the past 4 years I've worked on games that have gathered 60M+ visits, building everything from gameplay systems to the interfaces players interact with every day.

I care about code that is organised, fast and easy for a team to extend later, and about UI that feels polished and responsive. I work closely with owners and other developers, and I'm comfortable taking a feature from a rough idea to a finished, tested system.
